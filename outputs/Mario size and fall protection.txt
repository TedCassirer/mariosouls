Mario size and fall protection - Framework 0.3

Installed and tested on Mario QA, level 6.

Mario's visible model is 10% smaller. His movement body is now 44 cm wide and
105 cm tall, versus 80 cm and 150 cm before. The secondary native contact body
is 48 cm wide and 110 cm tall, versus 90 cm and 160 cm. Mario's own wall and
ceiling checks use the smaller dimensions too. Running speed, jump distances
and outgoing attack reach are unchanged.

While Mario controls movement, landing damage is reduced by 95%, capped at
5% of maximum health, and leaves at least 1 HP. Bottomless pits and native death
zones can still kill him. Native scripted falls are not covered by this policy.

Measured in game: 12 m fall = 5 HP damage; 25 m fall = 29 HP damage at 594 max HP.
Normal jump = 2.42 m with no stamina cost. Enemy melee still hurts and knocks
Mario back. Enemy bodies and closed doors remain solid; bonfire healing, Leave
and jumping work. Switching Mario off restores the original human body.

The body reduction is capsule-based, not a bone-by-bone hitbox rewrite. Projectile,
grab and boss-specific avoidance margins have not been tested in this update.

These settings belong to the shared framework's character descriptor. Mario's
values live in framework/characters/mario64/character.json. Changing them requires
rebuilding and testing; editing the installed manifest alone does not hot reload.

Mario QA was saved at the bonfire at full health and the game closed normally.
Controls are yours. During an earlier candidate launch, the main level-65 save
was briefly selected accidentally and immediately quit, with no movement, Mario
activation or gameplay checks. Its playtime increased by 36 seconds. All gameplay
tests used Mario QA; pre-test save backups are retained.
