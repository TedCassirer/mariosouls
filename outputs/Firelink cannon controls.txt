FIRELINK CANNON — FRAMEWORK 0.9.0

Installed for Mario mode. Look beside Firelink's central bonfire, toward the ruined arches.

Controller:
A beside the pink Bob-omb Buddy: unlock the cannon.
A beside the cannon: enter.
Left stick: aim. A: fire. Y: leave without firing.
Release the entry button before firing.

Keyboard:
Q: talk / enter / leave.
WASD: aim. Space: fire.

The cannon uses original SM64 opening actions and libsm64 aiming/flight, with
meshes, textures and Buddy animation loaded from your local ROM. The framework
handles interaction, camera and Dark Souls collision. The Buddy's unlock/dialogue
is a framework interaction, not the full SM64 NPC system.

Tested on Mario QA in Asylum and Firelink: entry, camera, launch, landing,
cancel, menu interruption and reload. Existing native health/fall damage remains.
Live tests used keyboard; the controller was disconnected. Cannon flight can
reach awkward ledges or leave the intended level. Every aim direction is not tested.

Mario QA is saved near the cannon. The game is closed. The temporary Asylum
cannon is disabled. Talk to the Buddy once to unlock the Firelink cannon.
