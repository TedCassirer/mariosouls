MarioSouls - Fire Flower (framework 0.8)

Select a Pyromancy Flame in either hand. Mario wears a white hat/shirt and red
overalls, and holds a Fire Flower. Switching away restores ordinary Mario.

Attune Fireball at a bonfire, then select it in your spell slot.
Right hand: B or RB on controller; left mouse on keyboard/mouse.
Left hand: RT on controller; Tab on keyboard/mouse.
With a left-hand shield instead, RT/Tab remains parry.
A/Space still interacts at a prompt and jumps elsewhere. Y remains an interaction alias.

Casting uses the native spell's remaining uses and damage. Bonfire rest replenishes
uses. Empty spell charges cannot produce free fireballs. Empty right-hand equipment
still allows Mario's own attacks, including when the flower is in his left hand.
Use one-handed stance. Two-handing, sorcery and miracles are not implemented here.

The inventory/HUD still calls the item Pyromancy Flame and uses its original icon.
The held flower, costume and throw poses are the new presentation. Native projectile
origin is retained; the animation is not full native-skeleton retargeting.

Verified on Mario QA: right/left Fireball kills, native souls and charge limits,
failed empty-charge cast, outfit switching and ordinary jump without stamina cost.
Controller mappings passed automated checks, but the controller was disconnected
during this test. Other spells/upgrades/bosses were not individually tested.
Mario QA was saved at the courtyard bonfire with flame right, shield left and
Fireball replenished. The level-65 character was never loaded.
