Mario collision fix / Souls Character Framework 0.4.1

Installed and tested on Mario QA.

Fixed: attacks check the enemy body surface rather than its center.
Fixed: the Asylum side exit no longer has a stale invisible barrier.
Preserved: smaller Mario body/model, reduced fall damage, movement and combat balance.

Verified: Asylum Demon combo 19 + 19 + 61 HP, jump kick 46 HP; side exit and stairs passable; closed main door solid; hollow damage/death/souls; free jumping; native incoming damage and bonfire healing.

Game is closed at the bonfire with Mario QA at full health. Launch normally and press F6 to activate Mario.

To restore this version later, close Dark Souls and run Restore Mario collision fix checkpoint.cmd in this folder. It restores the mod and corrected Asylum cache without restoring character saves.
Older checkpoint helpers intentionally restore older versions and can bring the old bugs back.

Other areas and every attack were not retested. Collision remains capsule-based, not an exact per-bone rewrite. The shared code supports future characters; Wind Waker Link was not live-tested during this Mario fix.
