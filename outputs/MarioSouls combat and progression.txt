MARIOSOULS — EXPERIMENTAL COMBAT AND PROGRESSION
Local PTDE build, 11 October 2026

IMPLEMENTED

Mario has his own physical attack power. Held weapon damage, weapon scaling and weapon attack effects are excluded from Mario's attack packet. The packet enters Dark Souls' damage calculation before enemy defenses; the numbers below are attack power, not direct HP damage. Native enemy health, deaths and soul rewards remain in use.

Power = 100 + 10 × upgrade level + Strength bonus + Dexterity bonus.
Upgrade levels are clamped to 0–15. Strength bonuses per point are:
  1–10: 0; 11–20: +3; 21–40: +2; 41–99: +0.5.
Dexterity contributes half the corresponding Strength bonus.

Examples:
  STR13 / DEX13, +0: 113.5 power.
  STR20 / DEX20, +8: 225 power.
  STR40 / DEX40, +15: 355 power.
  STR99 / DEX99, +15: 399.25 power.

Vitality and Endurance continue to use native HP and stamina. Stats and upgrades do not change SM64 movement speed, acceleration or jump distances. Walking, running and navigation jumps have no Mario combat stamina charge.

Move                   Power multiplier   Base stamina
First punch                  0.65              8
Second punch                 0.65              8
Finishing kick               1.10             16
Jump kick                    0.95             18
Dive                         1.15             24
Slide kick                   1.00             20
Stomp                        0.90             16
Ground pound                 1.65             30

Each punch and finishing kick has its own attack window and enemy-hit set. Prolonged contact cannot repeatedly damage the same enemy within one attack. Dive-to-slide and ground-pound-to-landing remain one attack each. A committed dive still costs stamina when it immediately collides with a wall.

Insufficient stamina: the movement/animation can continue, but the attack deals no damage and consumes no partial cost. Automatic stomps are charged only when eligible enemy contact occurs. An exhausted stomp produces neither damage nor rebound. A rebound cannot start another automatic stomp until Mario lands.

Ground pound has a 1.65-metre contact radius, a maximum of three enemies per attack, and approximately 0.4 seconds of landing recovery. There is no height-based attack bonus. Native poise damage is deliberately small and limited per enemy to prevent repeated contacts from stacking continuous stagger. This is an initial balance model, not a complete boss-by-boss balance pass.

EQUIPMENT WEIGHT

The enabled prototype adds 1% stamina cost per unit of equipped weapon/armor weight, capped at +50%, rounded up to a whole stamina point. It also extends the attack regeneration delay by 8 ms per weight unit, up to a total delay of 1 second. Movement physics remain unchanged.

The test character's equipment weighs 17.4. Its resulting costs are:
  punches 10 each; finishing kick 19; jump kick 22; dive 29;
  slide kick 24; stomp 19; ground pound 36.
The weight penalty is configurable. Full comparisons across light, medium and maximum-defense armor sets remain future tuning work.

HEALTH AND HAZARDS

Native Dark Souls HP is the authoritative health pool. SM64's separate health and invulnerability timer cannot create a second damage pool or extra protection. Native HP loss triggers Mario's hurt response. Ordinary jumps do not grant invulnerability. Native death releases Mario control and uses the normal respawn sequence.

Mario movement bypassed native fall tracking, so the integration now measures a fall and applies damage once to native HP. Current custom settings: drops up to 8 metres are safe; intermediate drops increase toward 50% maximum HP; 20 metres is lethal. These are Mario-specific testing rules, not a claim to reproduce Dark Souls' original fall formula. The Asylum's destructible arena-floor collision now follows its native intact/broken state.

VERIFIED IN THE REAL GAME

Only the existing level-4 character named test was used. The level-65 character was not loaded. Save backups were retained; no shared save was restored. Enemy AI and defenses remained active.

- Melee damage, Mario knockback, healing from 549 to 594 at the bonfire, lethal enemy damage totaling 594 HP, and normal respawn at 594 HP.
- A projectile caused one 35-HP decrease while Mario was rising in a double jump, followed by airborne hurt reaction. No combat stamina was charged for the jumps.
- Fall checks: 6 metres caused 0 damage; 12 metres caused one 99-HP decrease; a 21-metre drop reached the lethal threshold and caused native death. The lethal-fall runner lost focus during follow-up; subsequent observation confirmed respawn.
- A low-power punch caused 6 HP damage and cost 8 stamina with both a broken sword held and an empty hand held. Native weapon selection changed through the normal game control.
- All eight moves have attributed native damage results. At fixed Mario power 20, hollow results included punch 6, jump kick 12, dive 15, slide kick 13, stomp 11 and ground pound 24. Against the boss, the low-power finishing kick dealt 2. These cross-target values are not directly comparable.
- Base-cost checks matched actual stamina loss: punch 8, combo 32, jump kick 18, dive 24, slide kick 20, stomp 16, ground pound 30. A sustained-button punch hit once. Recorded enemy/attack pairs contained no repeated contact damage.
- An exhausted-stomp check used a temporary 100-stamina requirement against the character's 95 maximum: one unpaid stomp attempt, zero HP damage, zero stamina charge and no rebound. The normal cost was restored to 16.
- At fixed power 100, a 65-power punch dealt 10 to the armored enemy and 17 to the Asylum boss; the hollow's 44 HP was exhausted. The boss retaliated for 146 HP in the three-hit test.
- With the same STR13/DEX13 character, upgrade +15 produced a 171.275-power punch and 87 actual damage to the armored enemy.
- Simulated mid/late power profiles 225 and 355 produced 91 and 166 punch damage to the boss. These used the fixed-power test override; native character levels/stats were not changed.
- With normal +0 stat scaling and the weight penalty enabled, the STR13/DEX13 character dealt 13 punch damage to the armored enemy and paid exactly 10 stamina. The full combo paid exactly 39. Three hollow kills awarded 72 native souls in total, and bonfire rest restored the encounter.
- Final regression: Mario remained visible at the bonfire, resumed after Leave, jumped and landed normally. He was left at the bonfire with 594 HP, 95 stamina, level 4 and 72 souls. The test harness is disarmed.

Some earlier reports are deliberately retained as failed or inconclusive attempts. In particular, early development builds had an additional native 20-stamina action debit and sampled regeneration noise. Both were fixed. Use the final measurements and build identifiers, not those early totals, for balance decisions.

TESTING SETTINGS AND FUTURE UPGRADES

Normal enabled settings: BasePower=100, UpgradePower=10, UpgradeLevel=0, StatScaling=1, FixedPower=0, EquipmentCostPerWeight=0.01.

The adjacent Mario power settings.ps1 changes testing settings while the game is running. Example from PowerShell in this folder:
  & '.\Mario power settings.ps1' -UpgradeLevel 3
Run without arguments to return to the normal +0 settings.
FixedPower overrides the entire power formula when greater than zero.

These settings are global to Mario mode. Upgrade level is not yet stored per character, and no materials are consumed. No glove inventory item, blacksmith upgrade menu, titanite consumption or save-format modification has been implemented.

Proposed next upgrade interface: a unique Power Gloves progression entry with a permanent per-character +0–15 level, independent of what is equipped. Use native blacksmith/material transactions, with shard, large-shard, chunk and slab tiers. Keep native progression gates meaningful. Material quantities, soul costs, persistence and rollback on failed transactions must be designed and tested before enabling that interface.

REMAINING LIMITS

This is an Asylum-tested prototype. Poison, toxic, lava, every environmental damage source, every weapon/status-effect combination and a full playthrough have not been validated. Bonfire healing was tested; the test character had no equipped Estus for an item-healing check. High-stat profiles were simulated without leveling the character. Long-term boss stagger and armor balance need broader playtesting.

Existing controller controls and Mario-visible interaction handoffs remain available. F6 toggles Mario; after death or loading, re-enable him on solid ground. See MarioSouls controls and notes.txt for controls, supported areas and restore instructions.
