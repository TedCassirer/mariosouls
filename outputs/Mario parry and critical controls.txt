MARIO: PARRIES AND CRITICAL ATTACKS

Equip a supported one-handed melee weapon and a parry-capable shield.

Parry: RT on controller, Tab on keyboard.
Backstab: get close behind an enemy, face it, release guard, then tap B/RB
          (left mouse button on keyboard/mouse).
Riposte: parry successfully, step close in front, release guard, then tap attack.
Guard + attack also submits an explicit native critical attempt.

Dark Souls decides whether the position, timing, shield and enemy permit it.
An ineligible attempt may become an ordinary swing. Parry does not grant blanket
invincibility. Wait for the full native recovery before moving or attacking again.

Mario stays visible throughout. Empty-hand combat keeps Mario's moveset;
unarmed criticals and empty-hand parries are not included in this update.
Critical poses are authored for Mario, but native paired enemy animations can
still clip against his smaller body.

Live verified on Mario QA: Longsword backstab and parry/riposte, Plank Shield
parry, correct native stamina, damage, soul rewards, and return to jumping/swinging.
Other melee families passed offline animation/attachment tests. Physical RT was
not tested in this pass; controller routing passed automated checks.
