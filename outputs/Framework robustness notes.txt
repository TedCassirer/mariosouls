SOULS CHARACTER FRAMEWORK 0.2 — ROBUSTNESS CHECKPOINT

Installed and tested on Mario QA, 11 October 2026.

The main change is ownership: Mario proposes movement; Dark Souls resolves its
native body; the framework reconciles that result before the next movement step.
Requests cannot be replayed or carry over into a menu, interaction or new actor.

What changed
- One movement request at a time, consumed once by native physics.
- Collision correction preserves animations and removes blocked momentum only.
- Enemy body contacts no longer create artificial guest floor ledges.
- Explicit control ownership for free movement, native actions and load/death.
- Automatic re-creation after a safe native actor reload; explicit F6 off stays off.
- Short input taps and native hurt events survive gaps between simulation ticks.
- Fixed origin-rebase mesh drift and a correction that could cancel stomp bounce.
- Shared ABI 2, tests and documentation; future characters inherit the host rules.

Verified on the final installed build
- Native quit/load automatically restores Mario on the new Mario QA actor.
- Four toggles, bonfire rest/healing/leave, then jumping again.
- Closed-door blocking and enemy body collision (minimum separation 0.85 m).
- 2.42 m navigation jumps, no jump stamina; punch costs 9 with current equipment.
- Hollow HP 44 to 0, one hit-feedback receipt and one stamina charge.
- Native melee damage, Mario hurt/knockback, and bonfire healing to 594 HP.
- Full offline framework suite, including origin shifts and bounce preservation.
- No motion rejection/timeout in the final game log. Mario QA saved; game closed.

This is a stronger foundation, not a claim that every glitch is fixed. Testing
was confined to the Asylum. Other areas, death/respawn, bosses, ladders, dialogue,
Estus, projectiles and destructibles need further live regression. The native
human collider and collision-cache coverage remain current limitations. The
controller was disconnected; its mapping tests pass, but physical play was not
retested. The existing movement speeds and combat balance were kept.
