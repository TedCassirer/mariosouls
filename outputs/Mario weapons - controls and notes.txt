MARIO WEAPONS - FRAMEWORK 0.5.0

Installed: one-handed straight swords and small/medium shields.
Equip items through the ordinary Dark Souls Equipment menu. The selected right
and left slots determine what Mario holds. F6 still toggles Mario mode.

Attack: B or RB on controller; left mouse on keyboard/mouse.
Block: hold LB on controller; hold right mouse on keyboard/mouse.
Jump: A / Space. Crouch: LT / Ctrl.
Interact: Y / Q. Use selected item: X / E.
Choose an empty right-hand slot to restore Mario punches, kicks and other moves.

Sword damage and attack stamina come from the actual equipped weapon, including
its native upgrade and stat calculation. Empty-hand attacks keep Mario's own
power and moves. Swords have grounded light swings, limited combo buffering,
one hit per enemy per swing, and cannot start without enough stamina. Holding
attack does not repeat endlessly. Ordinary navigation jumps remain free.

Shield protection, chip damage and stamina drain are native Dark Souls rules.
The displayed shield is not a separate collision mesh. Native HP is authoritative.
Mario keeps his locomotion and has fitted sword/shield arm poses.

First implementation limits: no parrying, two-handed moves, ranged weapons,
magic attacks or other weapon families. Use supported gear in one-handed stance.
Unsupported gear falls back to Mario attacks. Props can briefly disappear during
hurt reactions and scripted interactions. Temporary weapon buffs and every
upgrade/elemental/status combination have not been validated.

Verified on Mario QA only: sword hit, normal unarmed kill, native blocking and
chip damage, rear damage, exhausted attack rejection, correct rendered hands,
bonfire healing/leave, and free jumps. Hilt hit: 44 to 31 HP, cost20 stamina.
Unarmed punch: 44 to0 HP, cost9 stamina. These are individual test results.
Actual guard break is implemented and tested with isolated native packet cases;
it has not yet been forced in a live fight. No physical controller was connected
for this final pass; controller mapping/routing passed the executable tests.

Mario QA is saved at the bonfire with the Straight Sword Hilt and Plank Shield.
The level-65 character was never loaded. Game closed normally after testing.

RESTORE THIS VERSION
Close Dark Souls, then run Restore Mario weapons checkpoint.cmd in this folder.
It verifies the private runtime archive and backs up overwritten files first.
It does not change saves. Keep the project folder and your owned ROM in place.
The earlier debug checkpoint and all earlier checkpoint tags remain unchanged.
