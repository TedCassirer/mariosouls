MarioSouls hit feedback — installed 11 October 2026

Landed attacks now have a brief impact pause, a punchy contact sound, a small
gold/white burst and controller rumble. Heavier attacks have a stronger response.
The pause buffers buttons so taps are not lost. Missed swings do not trigger it.
Enemy simulation, attack damage, stamina costs and stagger balance are unchanged.

This is part of the shared character framework, so future characters inherit it.

Verified on Mario QA: a nonlethal punch, a lethal ground pound, missed swings,
bonfire rest/leave and jumping afterward. The controller was disconnected, so
physical rumble still needs an in-hand check. Your level-65 character was not used.

To tune it, open the game's DATA\mariosouls.ini and find [Feedback].
Volume and RumbleStrength accept 0–100. HitStop, Sound, Flash and Rumble each
accept 0 or 1. Set Enabled=0 to turn all added feedback off. Changes reload in
about two seconds. Normal attack and interaction controls are unchanged.
